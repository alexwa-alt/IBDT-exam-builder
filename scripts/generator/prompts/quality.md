# Quality bar

The Head of DT reviewed each round of practice questions. Her feedback:

1. **Round 1** used invented contexts (pinto carrier, bagasse box, cleaver, bracket, kiosk, lantern,
   fans). Verdict: content okay, but **she wants the case studies from the IB past papers, not invented
   ones**, and the **challenge was too low**.
2. **Round 2** used past-paper case studies (Tot·M, BIOPODs, Hippo Roller, Every Stool, Eames radio,
   Utility Barrow, LOTA+) with **added data tables** and more judgement parts: **significantly better**,
   but still needed **more stretch at the top end** for high attainers.
3. Rule that follows: **always base a question on a case study from an old-syllabus IB past paper**
   (product, context, facts), then write **new parts that target the 2027 syllabus**. Invent a fictional
   context only as a last resort and flag it clearly for review.
4. How stretch was added without breaking the rules:
   - a final 4-mark **Evaluate / Analyse / Justify** part needing a sustained, two-sided, supported judgement;
   - **data interpretation or a short calculation** inside the harder parts (compare two columns, work out
     a percentage, cost, percentile or ratio);
   - **synthesis across stimulus**: the answer only works if the student combines a figure/table with the text;
   - unfamiliar angle on a familiar product.
5. Do not use "Discuss" or "Determine" (old drafts did; the checker rejects them).
